Methodology and research connection

Data were loaded with pandas, checked for nulls, duplicates, ranges, column types, and category consistency. Since the raw data passed core validation, no observations were deleted or imputed. Two grouping variables were added for comparisons. This preserves the original measurements for reproducible SQL and exploratory analysis and forms the data preparation section of the final paper.
