A relationship worth investigating

More screen time, lower reported sleep quality.

The overall Pearson correlation is r = −0.747. However, screen time is essentially unrelated to reported sleep duration in this dataset (r = −0.002).

The research framework

PRIMARY QUESTION

How does daily screen time relate to sleep quality among college students?

The full dataset contains adults across eight occupations; the student subgroup is analyzed separately when relevant.

HYPOTHESES

H1: Greater screen time is associated with lower sleep quality.

H2: More pre-sleep phone use is associated with shorter sleep duration.

H3: Greater nighttime screen use is associated with later bedtimes; bedtime is not recorded in this dataset, so H3 cannot be tested.
